## Authority conformance vectors added (questions 3, 4, 5, 21)

Four authority questions — was the sub-agent authorized and by whom,
what exactly was it permitted to do, did it stay inside that, and who
else holds authority from the same grant — now have conformance vectors
in ``tests/conformance/auditor/test_authority_vectors.py``.

All four are registered as strict expected failures: the audit receipt
records that a delegation happened (``agent.delegated``) but carries no
authorization grant identifier (Q3/#5046), no permitted-action scope
(Q4/#5046), no boundary-compliance attestation (Q5/#5055), and no
grant-propagation record (Q21/#5047).  A companion test
(``test_q3_delegation_event_is_present``) confirms the delegation event
itself is present, separating the working half from the unanswered
question.  The auditor conformance score remains 3/21 (#5059).
