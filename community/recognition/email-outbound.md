# The mail the maintainer sends when a period closes

Sent from forte@bernstein.run to the people who cleared the soft gate in a
period and are not yet in `opt-ins.toml`. Short on purpose; the long version
is the pinned issue. Fill the angle brackets; do not add sections.

```
Subject: BRNSTN-PR-LNKD

Hi <name or login>,

Your pull requests landed in Bernstein over the last month
(<n> merged, <added> lines, the list is under the pinned issue:
<link to the periodic comment>). Roughly once every couple of weeks the
project page on LinkedIn names, in one shared post, the contributors who
want to be named, and says what they built. Details, and why, are here:
<link to the pinned issue>.

If you want in, reply to this mail with the subject unchanged and these
three lines in the body:

GITHUB=<login>
OPT_IN=YES
RECOMMENDATION=NO

Set RECOMMENDATION=YES if you would rather have a LinkedIn recommendation
from me (mostly useful if you are job-hunting right now). Please keep the
subject exactly as it is; it is what routes the mail.

Reply by <date, 20:00 Israel time> if you want to be in the next post;
after that you are simply in the one after.

Thanks for the work.
Alex
```
